# Social Media Sentiments and Consumer Trends 

The project investigates whether **expressive social discussion, particularly social media mentions peaks, is associated with increased brand demand**, and uses those findings to **inform more targeted crowd marketing strategies**.

This project forms part of a larger mixed methods research program examining how online brand discussions relate to consumer demand (funded by a think tank the ICDS). The analysis combines monthly social media sentiment data and Google search volume across 39 brands over two years, covering more than 270 million social media mentions.

**Main business objective of the project**: determine whether the volume and emotional character of online brand discussion contain actionable signals of consumer interest and whether these signals can inform more targeted digital marketing strategies.   

**Tools used**: social media monitoring platform (SCAN) for raw data sourcing, Excel for data cleaning, R for data validation, statistical analysis and data visualization

## Business problem and project background

The central business problem was whether brands should actively manage and stimulate expressive online discussion rather than simply seeking high volumes of neutral mentions and, if so, which forms of discussion are most relevant to generating demand.

The analysis addressed four business-specific questions:
* **Does online sentiment matter commercially?** Are positive and negative discussions associated with different levels of consumer interest than neutral brand discussion?
* **Does the intensity of discussion matter?** Are periods of unusually high expressive discussion rather than less significant levels of conversation associated with increases in brand search demand?
* **What type of online discussion should marketing strategies aim to stimulate?** Can combinations of sentiment characteristics be identified that are more closely associated with demand than generic increases in mention volume?
* **How can these relationships improve crowd marketing strategy?** Can empirical evidence be translated into more customized, theory-driven targeting of the types, intensity and timing of online discussion rather than relying on intuitive campaign design?
